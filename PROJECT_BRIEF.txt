--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. PROJECT PURPOSE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
inYice-Lite is a multi-tenant B2B and B2C order-bookkeeping platform for travel
agencies. The product focus is intentionally narrow:

- Company profile management
- Limited agency staff management
- Customer and vendor network management
- Order bookkeeping with full lifecycle tracking
- Full auditability of user actions and order changes

This scope excludes expense management, attendance, and shift management.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. COMPANY INFO
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Each agency manages its own company profile and operational identity, including
business details required for order and invoice operations.

- Company-level setup and ownership
- Company-linked orders, customers, and vendors
- Company-scoped records for complete tenant isolation

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. COMPANY STAFF (LIMITED TO 3 PER AGENCY)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Each agency can create and operate with only three unique staff users,
mapped to three business roles:

- Admin
- Sales
- Accounts

Rules:
- Agency accounts are limited to these three users/roles.
- The platform provider account can create unlimited employees.
- Staff actions are always captured in activity/audit logs.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. CUSTOMER & VENDOR DATA MODEL
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The platform supports both B2B agency-to-agency relationships and agency-local
B2C customer records.

4.1 B2B AGENCY RELATIONSHIPS
----------------------------
- A company can mark itself as a vendor for other agencies.
- Agencies can become each other's B2B customer/vendor counterpart.
- B2B links are explicit and controlled by business records.

4.2 B2C CUSTOMER OWNERSHIP
--------------------------
- Every agency keeps its own offline/direct customer database.
- B2C customer data is private to the owning agency.
- One agency cannot read or manage another agency's B2C customers.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. ORDER BOOKKEEPING & FULL LIFECYCLE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The core business flow is lifecycle-based order bookkeeping.

5.1 LIFECYCLE
-------------
Quote -> Order -> Confirm/Cancel -> Invoice/Refund/Void

Business meaning:
- Quote: Initial commercial offer.
- Order: Accepted quote and active processing state.
- Confirm: Operationally confirmed booking/order.
- Cancel: Order cancelled before financial completion.
- Invoice: Financial posting and customer billing.
- Refund: Reversal after invoicing where applicable.
- Void: Invoice/order invalidated as per control policy.

5.2 ORDER RECORDING
-------------------
- Booking reference and order number tracking
- Customer and vendor linkage
- Staff ownership (who created/updated)
- Notes and status history for audit-safe bookkeeping

5.3 GDS-DRIVEN FLIGHT DATA CAPTURE
----------------------------------
Order capture supports GDS text parsing for flight-related bookkeeping.
From GDS PNR content, the system can extract and structure:

- Booking reference (PNR)
- Passenger details and passenger type codes
- Segment-level flight details (airline, route, dates/times, status)
- GDS source markers (e.g., Sabre/Galileo)
- Ticket-related details used in order records

This parsed data is attached to order items to reduce manual entry and improve
accuracy in quote/order/confirmation/invoice stages.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. TENANT ISOLATION & PLATFORM VISIBILITY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Data visibility rules are strict:

- Agency A cannot access Agency B data.
- Agency B cannot access Agency A data.
- Each agency operates in an isolated tenant space for customers, vendors,
  and orders.
- Only the platform provider has cross-tenant/global visibility.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7. ACTIVITY LOGGING & AUDIT CONTROL
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
System logging is mandatory for accountability and operations review.

Track at minimum:
- User sign-in activity
- Order creation
- Order lifecycle transitions (Quote, Order, Confirm, Cancel,
  Invoice, Refund, Void)
- Order edits
- Order deletes

Audit goals:
- Who performed each action
- What changed
- When it changed

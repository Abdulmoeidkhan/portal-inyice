## Eligibility and account authority

The current connection requires an active paid agency, an active verified user, and an owner, admin or accounts role. Do not connect another person's account without authorization or share login credentials.

Your agency is responsible for the accuracy and lawful collection of records it enters and for managing staff access. Only provide information you are authorized to process and share.

## What the connection does

The connection can search customers, list outstanding invoices, retrieve customer statements, show cash and bank balances, and return profit reports. Tool results are limited to the authenticated user's agency. It cannot create or modify accounting records, make payments, capture portal screenshots, or publish social posts.

An AI service may misunderstand a request, omit information or generate an incorrect explanation. Check important figures against the underlying InYice records, including dates and currencies, before relying on an answer. The connection provides access to accounting information; it does not replace professional accounting, tax or legal advice.
